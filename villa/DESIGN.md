# Villa Aralia — Design Brief

A contemporary desert-modern villa carved into a rocky hillside. Horizontal cantilevered volumes, deep overhangs for shade, full-height glazing dissolving the line between interior and landscape.

## Site
- High desert hillside, mountain backdrop (Joshua Tree / Palm Springs vernacular)
- Single-story main volume with partial second-story bedroom wing cantilevered over the pool deck
- ~420 m² (~4,500 sq ft) conditioned area
- East-facing main view for sunrise; pool oriented west for sunset

## Massing
- Two stacked rectangular volumes, offset
- Lower volume: living, kitchen, dining, primary suite
- Upper volume: two guest suites, study, terrace
- Cantilever: 3.5m on the western edge over the infinity pool
- Floating effect via recessed plinth lit at night

## Materials (the style anchors — reuse in every prompt)
- **Travertine** — honed, warm cream, large-format
- **Board-formed concrete** — vertical grain, raw, charcoal
- **Blackened steel** — window frames, fireplace surround
- **Walnut** — interior millwork, ceiling soffits
- **Floor-to-ceiling bronze-tinted glass**
- **Vertical timber screens** — thermally modified oak, sun shading

## Program
- Sunken conversation pit by central fireplace
- Open kitchen with travertine waterfall island
- 25m linear infinity pool, dark plaster, mirror finish
- Internal courtyard with mature olive tree, exposed to sky
- Outdoor kitchen + fire pit on west deck
- Subterranean wine cellar accessed via floating walnut stair

## Palette
- Warm neutrals: cream, sand, taupe
- Accents: terracotta, deep teal (pool), brass fittings
- Greenery: olive, agave, ornamental grasses
