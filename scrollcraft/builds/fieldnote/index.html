§ 01 · The unwritten one

The thought you had walking to the station last Tuesday. The one that felt clear until the platform. That one.

§ 02 · The mark

One line and you have started.

Ink is Waterman Serenity Blue, drawn on 90 gsm cream, roughly to the length of a signature.

§ 03 · What it is

Ninety-six sewn pages,

bound in a card cover you will bend without regret,

and a paper the pen catches instead of skating on.

§ 04 · Three

Dot.

5 mm grid, faintest.
For plans, sketches, page layouts.

Ruled.

7 mm, warm grey.
For running prose and lecture notes.

Plain.

Nothing at all.
For the drawing you do not know yet.

Fieldnote is a small run of three notebooks. They ship in cotton wrap, undated, unbranded on the cover.

If one of the three fits how you already work, carry one.
