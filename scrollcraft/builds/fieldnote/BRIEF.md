# Fieldnote — brief

## 1. Vibe (3–5 words) + up to three references
Quiet, papery, decisive, hand-marked. References: the interior of a Muji stationery shop, a Bauhaus poster set at one weight, the endpaper of an unwritten Moleskine.

## 2. Scroll journey, section by section
1. A blank page waiting.
2. The thought I didn't write down.
3. A hand marks a line across the paper.
4. What a notebook actually is: paper, binding, format.
5. The three formats: dot, ruled, plain.
6. Carry one.

## 3. Energy curve
Very quiet start; a single loud mark in the middle; then quiet again to the close. Never raises its voice more than once.

## 4. Feeling curve (one line per act, added to as the score fills in)
1. **Stillness** — a single word at extreme scale sits alone on paper.
2. **Restraint** — a small paragraph, quiet, tucked into a corner. Not a headline.
3. **Marking** *(PEAK)* — a nib crosses the whole viewport, drawing a real ink line as the reader scrolls. The turn from paper-thinking to paper-marking.
4. **Weight** — a three-line stanza wipes in and holds. The heaviest reading moment.
5. **Choice** — three format names arrive at three scales; the choice reads as three words, not three cards.
6. **Resolve** — the smallest type on the site. One underlined line. No button island.

**Peak:** *the nib crossing the page, act 3.* The sentence a visitor would say to a friend: "there's this site where scrolling literally draws an ink line across a piece of paper." Peak lives in act 3 and gets the largest scroll span on the page.

## 5. One thing this site does that no other does
Scroll is a nib. A real SVG stroke draws across the entire viewport, ink following the reader's wheel, ending on a signed underline. The signature move is coded in the page, driven from `--sc-p`, not a kit device.

## 6. Aesthetic family
**Editorial** (paper ground, restraint, folio-style small chrome). Not premium-minimal (would default to dark canvas and one accent, which is the template trap already flagged in uniqueness.md §1). Not maximalist — a notebook that shouts is not a notebook.

## 7. One unbroken world or distinct scenes?
Distinct scenes. This is not a world to fly through; it is six moments a page holds and lets go of. Worldflight would be a lie.

## 8. Assets already on hand
None. Fully self-generated. Because the grammar is typographic poster, this is a feature: the page's imagery is its type. No photography needed, no clip generation needed, no KIE_AI spend.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where scrolling draws a real ink line across a piece of paper.

## Authored silence
Between act 2 (Restraint) and act 3 (Marking), one viewport of paper with nothing on it, so the nib arrives against an empty sheet. That is silence on purpose, not dead scroll — the verification pass would otherwise flag it.
